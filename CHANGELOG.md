# Changelog

Dated entries, newest first. Legal content changes name the jurisdiction and the date the content reflects.

## 2026-10-29 — v1.1

- Toolkit pages simplified: the boxed cards (tool overview, downloads, step accordions, example cards, party and fact panels, path chips, layer buttons, region corridors, side notes) replaced by plain text with hairline separators; download buttons became text links. No content or interaction removed. Corridor bars on the Legal landscape page now render.
- Tables updated to the 29 October revision: Tool 1 Step 7 “No” assigns Tier 2 (the Tier 2A/2B labels are withdrawn).
- Worked examples revised on the authors’ review: open deposit (Tier 2 with the linkage route and disclosure risk recorded as assessment factors); Nigeria (controller-of-major-importance status to be verified against the NDPC criteria); South Africa and Kenya (the transfer route assumed for the scenario is one of several, to be confirmed by counsel; Kenya’s serving-copy, transfer and research-permit requirements distinguished); Libya (video is directly identifying; its biometric status is regime-specific); Egypt (Executive Regulations under Decree 816/2025 cited; the scenario stipulates that the licence is unavailable).

- Tables updated to the 27 October revision: Tool 1 Step 7 “No” now assigns Tier 2A (de-identified, no linkage mechanism) or Tier 2B (key retained) instead of ending in specialist review; Example 1 (open deposit) returns to “Proceed”. Typographical fixes adopted by the authors removed from the local-corrections list.

- Tools 1–4 updated to the authors' revised Supplementary Tables 6–9 (26 October 2026): applicability checkpoint and tier-confirmation rule in Tool 1; controlled-access (A) and public-release (B) pathways and the statutory-authorisation step 7A in Tool 2; the Annexure 1 §7.2 exception reproduced in Tool 3; public-release applicability (Step 1A) in Tool 4. The sub-tier 1b, the participant-status step, the matrix-class step and the split lawful-basis step proposed in the review draft are withdrawn.
- Tier framework aligned with MDTAA Annexure 1 §3 (five tiers).
- Workflow figure, PDFs and all sixteen worked examples rebuilt on the revised steps; Example 1 (open deposit) now ends in "specialist review required" because Tool 1 assigns no tier to de-identified, non-linkable participant-level data.
- Typographical corrections applied to the site copy of the tables and reported to the authors (stray "? ?", "inNeurodata", a stray closing quote at Tool 2 Step 9B and Tool 4 Step 12, "9," and "4," list labels, "SupplementaryTables", duplicated clause in Tool 1 Step 4, text held in empty nested tables at Tool 2 Steps 8A, 9A, 11A and 13 and Tool 3 Step 4).
