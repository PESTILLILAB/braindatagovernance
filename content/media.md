## Logo

The mark is a compass rose tilted 20 degrees: four long points in the four colours and four short ones in ink, with a thin ring that the long points break through. Use the SVG wherever you can; the PNGs are for places that cannot take vector files. Below about 60 pixels the thin arms and ring are lost, so for headers, avatars and icons use `mark-small.svg`: the same rose with wider arms, upright, on a white disc. The favicon is derived from it.

Keep clear space around the mark of at least the width of one compass point, and do not recolour, rotate it further, add a second enclosure or effects to it. On dark backgrounds use the dark version; where only one colour is available, use the mono version.
