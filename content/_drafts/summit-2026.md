Rio de Janeiro, December 2026. The BRIDGE community meets to review the first instruments, the Neurodata Governance Toolkit and the legal landscape of 93 jurisdictions, and to set the agenda for an international governance framework for brain and mental health research.
{: .lead}

## Who it is for

Researchers who share or receive brain data, data stewards and repository managers, lawyers and data-protection officers, ethics-committee members, regulators, funders, and people with lived experience of brain and mental-health conditions. The summit follows the regional workshops in Rio de Janeiro (2024) and Marrakesh (2025) and the global workshop in Johannesburg (2025).
