Global Brain Governance is built in the open. There are several ways to take part, whatever your role.
{: .lead}

::cards
- **Use the products** — Run a planned collaboration through the toolkit, assemble the MDTAA modules it selects, and bring the record to your legal, ethics and repository offices. The explorer, PDFs and worked examples are free to use under CC BY 4.0. [Open the toolkit](/toolkit/)
- **Pilot the toolkit and report back** {#pilot} — The tools have not yet been evaluated in prospective pilots. Collaborations that use them are asked to tell us what worked, what did not, and where the law or the questions were unclear. Reports become worked examples and improvements. [Open an issue](https://github.com/pestillilab)
- **Review a jurisdiction** {#review} — Lawyers, data-protection officers, repository managers and ethics-committee members can review the coding of their jurisdiction, propose corrections, or add a jurisdiction not yet covered. Changes to legal content are reviewed by a regional co-author before they are merged. [Volunteer in an issue](https://github.com/pestillilab)
- **Contribute to the code and content** {#contribute} — The site, the explorer and the data are on GitHub. Corrections, new worked examples, translations, regime modules and improvements to the explorer are welcome as pull requests. [github.com/pestillilab](https://github.com/pestillilab)
- **Attend an event** {#events} — BRIDGE holds regional and global workshops and a global summit where the instruments are designed, tested and taught. Past workshops took place in Rio de Janeiro (2024), Marrakesh (2025) and Johannesburg (2025); the Global Summit is in Rio de Janeiro in December 2026. [bridge.incf.org](https://bridge.incf.org/)
- **Join as a member institution** {#join} — Universities, hospitals, repositories and national brain projects join by naming two representatives, a data steward or research-office lead and a scientist, and agreeing to demonstrate at least one instrument on a real collaboration. Members shape the roadmap and are the first adopters. [Write to us](mailto:pestilli@utexas.edu)
- **Become a funder or partner** {#fund} — The next products are scoped as packages a funder or partner can take on: ezGov, the legal handbook, new regions, machine-readable governance, a model registry, pilots. [Support the work](/support/)
