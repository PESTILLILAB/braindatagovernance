Two products: the Neurodata Governance Toolkit, available now, and ezGov, in development. Each is published with a version, the date to which its legal content was verified, a citation and an open licence, and can be used, adapted and redistributed, including commercially, with attribution.
{: .lead}

::products

## Product roadmap {#roadmap}

Each product follows the same path: drafted with the regional legal scholars, demonstrated by member institutions on live collaborations, revised, then released with a version number. The Toolkit is the first through that path. The items below are in the order we expect to work on them; dates are indicative.

::roadmap

## Versions and dates

The law moves, so every product carries a version number and the date to which its legal content was checked; changes are recorded on GitHub. The Toolkit's legal content is current to {{site.legal_content_date}}. Anything still awaiting confirmation by a legal reviewer in its region is marked [verify] inside the product.
